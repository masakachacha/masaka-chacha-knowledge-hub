What Is Psychology?

Psychology is the scientific study of mind and behaviour. It investigates how people think, learn, feel, remember, make decisions and interact with others. Psychologists use different research methods to understand human behaviour and mental processes.

Major Branches of Psychology

Cognitive psychology studies processes such as thinking, memory and problem-solving. Developmental psychology examines changes across the lifespan. Social psychology investigates how people influence one another. Educational psychology studies learning and teaching, while clinical psychology focuses on assessing and helping people with psychological difficulties.

Learning and Motivation

Learning involves acquiring or changing knowledge, skills and behaviour through experience, practice and other processes. Motivation influences the direction, effort and persistence of behaviour. In education, clear goals, useful feedback and a supportive learning environment can help learners remain engaged.

Emotions and Human Behaviour

Emotions can influence attention, memory, relationships and decision-making. Human behaviour, however, is usually shaped by several interacting factors, including personal experiences, biological processes, social relationships and the surrounding environment.

Why Psychology Matters

Psychology can help teachers understand learners, organisations improve teamwork and individuals develop better self-awareness. Scientific psychological knowledge also encourages us to question stereotypes and avoid judging people based on a single behaviour or experience.
