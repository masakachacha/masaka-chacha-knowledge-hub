What Is Philosophy?

Philosophy is the systematic study of fundamental questions about reality, knowledge, morality, reasoning and human existence. It encourages people to examine ideas carefully rather than accept every claim without questioning it.

Major Branches of Philosophy

Metaphysics studies reality and existence. Epistemology examines knowledge and how we know what we know. Ethics studies right and wrong. Logic examines the principles of sound reasoning.

Why Philosophy Matters

Philosophy develops critical thinking, careful argumentation and intellectual humility. It helps us recognise weak arguments, investigate assumptions and make more thoughtful decisions.
