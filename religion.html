What Is Religion?

Religion refers to systems of beliefs, practices, values and traditions through which people understand the sacred, spiritual realities and questions about human existence. Religions differ in their teachings, scriptures, rituals and views of the world.

The Study of Religion

The academic study of religion examines religious beliefs, history, texts, rituals and their influence on individuals and societies. Researchers may use history, anthropology, sociology, philosophy and textual analysis to understand religious traditions.

Religion and Critical Thinking

Critical thinking encourages us to examine religious claims carefully, understand their historical and cultural contexts, distinguish evidence from interpretation and represent other people's beliefs fairly. Respectful inquiry does not require agreement with every belief.

Religion, Ethics and Society

Religious traditions have influenced moral teachings, art, education, laws and community life in many societies. Their effects vary across historical periods and cultures, making careful study important for understanding humanity.
