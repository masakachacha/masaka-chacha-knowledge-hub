What Is Science?

Science is a systematic way of studying the natural and physical world through observation, investigation, evidence and testing. It helps us develop explanations about how things work and revise those explanations when stronger evidence becomes available.

Major Branches of Science

Biology studies living organisms. Physics investigates matter, energy, motion and forces. Chemistry examines substances and how they change. Earth science studies our planet, while astronomy investigates objects and phenomena beyond Earth.

The Scientific Method

Scientific investigations often involve asking a question, making observations, developing a hypothesis, gathering evidence and analysing results. Researchers then communicate their findings so that other people can examine and test them.

Science and Everyday Life

Science contributes to medicine, agriculture, communication, transport, environmental protection and technology. Scientific knowledge is most useful when it is applied responsibly and its limitations are understood.

Science and Critical Thinking

A scientific attitude encourages curiosity, careful reasoning and openness to new evidence. Instead of accepting every claim immediately, we ask what evidence supports it and whether the claim can be tested.
